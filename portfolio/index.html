A little about how I work

I graduated from the Egypt‑Japan University of Science and Technology with a degree in Computer Engineering, and I've spent the years since moving between four things I care about: building AI systems that run efficiently on real hardware, shipping full‑stack and mobile products end to end, working inside enterprise content‑management platforms, and teaching programming to people just starting out.

My favourite projects sit at the intersection of constraint and creativity — like getting a quantized language model to answer questions offline, in under four seconds, on a laptop GPU. I care about writing software that is useful outside a demo, and about explaining how it works clearly enough that someone else could build the next version.
